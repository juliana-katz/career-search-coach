# Leveling realism — source × target framework

The user's "realistic landing title" depends on BOTH their source company AND their target company. Don't apply a single "lateral or +1" rule — apply it relative to **company tier**.

## Company tiers (rough)

| Tier | Examples | Title behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Mega-cap public / FAANG / frontier AI** | Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Stripe, OpenAI, Anthropic, Figma | Titles **deflated** — their Senior IC = Director at smaller co. Director at FAANG = senior leadership. VP = exec |
| **Mid-large public co / late-stage unicorn** | Salesforce, Atlassian, Notion, Linear, Mercury, Brex, Chime, Pinterest, Reddit | Titles roughly **face value**. Director means Director. VP = function head |
| **Profitable Series C–D unicorn** | Calendly, Webflow, Zapier, Loom, Honeybook | Titles roughly face value; some inflation at exec level |
| **Series B–C startup** | Booksy-stage co's; pre-IPO Series C | Mild title inflation; VP is often a small-team head |
| **Series A–B startup (early)** | < 50 employees | Significant title inflation; "VP" can mean "the first marketing hire" |

## Source × Target matching matrix

When the user is currently at tier X and considering tier Y, the *realistic landing title* is:

### Source = FAANG / frontier AI

| Target | Realistic landing |
|---|---|
| FAANG / frontier AI | Lateral title or +1 |
| Mid-large public co | +1 title (Sr PMM @ Google → Director @ Salesforce) |
| Series C–D unicorn | +1 or +2 title (Sr PMM @ Google → Sr Director / Head of X @ Calendly) |
| Series A–B startup | +2 title or "VP / Head of" (Sr PMM @ Google → VP Marketing @ Series B) |

### Source = mid-large public co / late-stage unicorn

| Target | Realistic landing |
|---|---|
| FAANG / frontier AI | -1 title or lateral (Director @ Notion → Sr PMM @ Google; only +1 with exceptional scope/pedigree) |
| Mid-large public co | Lateral title |
| Series C–D unicorn | Lateral or +1 (Director @ Notion → Director or Sr Director @ Calendly) |
| Series A–B startup | +1 (Director @ Notion → VP or Head of X @ Series B) |

### Source = Series C–D unicorn / profitable startup

| Target | Realistic landing |
|---|---|
| FAANG / frontier AI | -1 title (Director @ Calendly → Sr PMM @ Google) |
| Mid-large public co | Lateral (Director @ Calendly → Director @ Salesforce) |
| Series C–D unicorn | Lateral |
| Series A–B startup | +1 (Director @ Calendly → VP or Head of X) |

### Source = Series A–B early-stage startup

| Target | Realistic landing |
|---|---|
| FAANG / frontier AI | -2 title (VP @ 30-person startup → Senior PMM @ Google) — scope re-set |
| Mid-large public co | -1 (VP @ 30-person → Director @ Salesforce) |
| Series C–D | Lateral (VP @ Series B → VP or Head of X @ Series C) |
| Series A–B | Lateral |

## Hard rules

1. **Two-step jumps are rare.** A Director at Co A → VP at Co B (skipping Sr Director) requires exceptional context: huge name-brand pedigree, massive demonstrated scope at smaller co, internal leverage at the target, or unique skill match. Don't surface unless asked.

2. **Post-layoff users have LESS negotiation leverage.** A clean lateral is usually the realistic ceiling. Stretch leveling typically requires the user to be currently employed and selectively choosing.

3. **Prestige of source company can pull the equivalent up.** Coming from Adobe / Stripe / Anthropic / Apple gives more title flexibility at smaller cos than coming from a no-name company with the same title.

4. **Function pivots reset leveling.** If the user is moving from Brand Marketing → Product Marketing, or Engineering Manager → IC Staff, expect a "lateral by title, smaller scope" landing. Sometimes a step down to break into the new function.

5. **Within-FAANG moves usually maintain or increase level.** FAANG-to-FAANG is the safest title-preservation move.

## When in doubt

Ask the user: **"What's the title you'd be willing to take?"** If their answer is unrealistic given the source × target matrix, flag it directly: *"That would be a +2 jump given your current tier — it can happen but rarely. Want me to also include lateral / +1 options as backup?"*

## How to apply

Before surfacing any role to the user's Tier 1/Tier 2 hit list:
1. Identify the user's source company tier
2. Identify the target company tier
3. Look up the realistic landing in the matrix
4. If the role's title is +2 above the realistic landing → flag as stretch
5. If the role's title is -1 or worse → flag as down-leveling (may not be acceptable to user)
