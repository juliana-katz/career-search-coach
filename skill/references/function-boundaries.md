# Function boundaries — don't conflate adjacent functions

**The most common skill failure mode is confusing partner functions.**

A senior search depends on matching the user's *actual function* to the role's *actual function* — not on keyword overlap. These functions are adjacent partners, not interchangeable.

When in doubt, read the JD body. Title alone is misleading.

---

## Marketing functions

| Function | What they do | Sample titles | Partners with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product Marketing (PMM)** | Positioning, messaging, GTM, launches, packaging, monetization | Director/VP Product Marketing | Product, Engineering, Sales |
| **Brand Marketing** | Brand strategy, positioning narrative, campaign concepts, marketing budget | Director/VP Brand Marketing | Creative, PR, Comms |
| **Creative Operations** | Operations leadership of creative teams — workflow, briefing, agency mgmt, in-house studio ops, production governance | Director/Sr Director Creative Operations | Brand, Production, Agencies |
| **Creative Strategy & Production** | Strategic creative direction + production execution; cross-functional org leader of Brand/Strategy/Social/Creative Ops/Production | Sr Director Creative Strategy & Production | CMO, Brand, Product |
| **Integrated Marketing** | Campaign orchestration across channels (paid + organic + lifecycle + comms) | Director Integrated Marketing | All marketing functions |
| **Growth / Performance Marketing** | Paid acquisition, lifecycle, attribution, CAC/LTV, channel optimization | Head/VP Growth Marketing | Product, Data, Finance |
| **Lifecycle / CRM Marketing** | Email, push, SMS, in-app — engagement and retention across customer journey | Director Lifecycle Marketing | Growth, Product |
| **Creative Director (IC)** | Hands-on creative leadership — concept, copy, visuals — for specific brand/campaign | Creative Director (often IC at FAANG without "Senior/Head/Group" prefix) | Brand, Production |
| **Content Marketing** | Editorial strategy, content production, SEO/AEO, owned media | Director Content Marketing, Head of Editorial | Brand, Demand Gen, PR |
| **Comms / PR** | External communications, press, executive thought leadership, crisis | Director Communications, VP Comms | Brand, Exec leadership |
| **Demand Gen** | Top-of-funnel campaigns, lead acquisition, paid channels for pipeline | Director Demand Gen | Growth, Sales |

## Product Management functions

| Function | What they do | Sample titles | Career track |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product Manager (PM, IC)** | Roadmap, requirements, prioritization, cross-functional coordination for a product/feature | Product Manager, Senior PM | IC track |
| **Group Product Manager (GPM)** | Manages a small team of PMs on a product area | Group PM | Hybrid IC/management |
| **Director of Product / Sr Director / VP Product** | Manages PMs + product area; owns roadmap for a product line or business unit | Director Product, VP Product | Management track |
| **CPO** | Owns all of product across the company | Chief Product Officer | Executive |
| **Technical PM (TPM)** | PM with deeper technical scope — APIs, infrastructure, dev tools | Technical PM, Sr TPM | IC track (often) |
| **Growth PM** | PLG mechanics — onboarding flows, activation, monetization loops, experimentation | Growth PM, Sr Growth PM | IC track |
| **Platform PM** | Internal platform / infrastructure / developer experience PM | Platform PM | IC track |
| **Product Operations / Product Ops** | Process, tooling, metrics infrastructure for the product org | Product Ops Manager, Director Product Ops | Operations track |
| **Principal PM** | IC track senior — sets product strategy without managing people | Principal PM | IC track |

**Critical distinction:** Product Marketing (PMM) ≠ Product Management (PM). They partner closely but do different jobs. PMM owns positioning + GTM; PM owns roadmap + execution. A "Director of Product Marketing" and a "Director of Product Management" are NOT the same role.

**Consumer PM vs Enterprise PM:** very different. Consumer PM owns end-user features (signup, engagement, monetization); Enterprise PM owns API design, admin features, sales-driven roadmaps. Read JD carefully — both might be titled "Senior PM."

## Engineering functions

| Function | What they do | Sample titles | Career track |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Software Engineer (IC)** | Writes/reviews code; ships features | SWE, Senior SWE | IC track |
| **Staff Engineer** | Senior IC; owns systems/architecture for a domain | Staff SWE | IC senior track |
| **Principal Engineer** | Even more senior IC; sets technical direction across teams | Principal Engineer | IC very senior |
| **Distinguished Engineer / Fellow** | IC top-of-track; influences company-wide direction | Distinguished Engineer | IC executive equivalent |
| **Engineering Manager (EM)** | Manages a team of ICs; people management + delivery | EM, Senior EM | Management track |
| **Sr EM / Director of Engineering** | Manages EMs + multi-team scope | Sr EM, Director Engineering | Management track |
| **VP Engineering / SVP / CTO** | Org-wide leadership; technical strategy | VP Eng, CTO | Executive |
| **Tech Lead (TL) / Tech Lead Manager (TLM)** | IC-leaning lead, sometimes with light management responsibility | Tech Lead, TLM | Hybrid |
| **SRE / Production Engineer** | Reliability, on-call, infrastructure ops | SRE, Sr SRE, Director SRE | Specialty (parallel to SWE track) |
| **Security Engineer** | Application/infra security | Security Engineer, Director Security | Specialty |
| **Data Engineer / ML Engineer** | Data infrastructure / ML systems | Data Engineer, ML Engineer | Specialty |

**Critical distinction:** IC track vs Management track diverge at the senior levels. A Staff Engineer at Google is NOT a "less senior" Director of Engineering — they're parallel-track equivalents. Don't recommend someone on the IC track switch to management roles (or vice versa) without confirming that's their actual intent.

**Engineering Manager (EM) ≠ Project Manager ≠ Product Manager:** Common outside-tech confusion. EM manages engineers + delivery. PM owns product roadmap. Project Manager owns timelines/coordination across functions (rare title at big tech; more common at agencies/consulting).

## Program & Operations functions

| Function | What they do | Sample titles |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical Program Manager (TPM)** | Drives cross-team technical programs; PM + EM hybrid responsibility for delivery, not roadmap | TPM, Sr TPM, Director TPM |
| **Program Manager** | Drives non-technical cross-functional programs (launches, GTM, BizOps initiatives) | Program Manager, Sr Program Manager |
| **Chief of Staff** | Founder/CEO's right-hand — runs strategic initiatives, special projects, exec ops | Chief of Staff, Sr CoS |
| **Strategy & Operations / BizOps** | Strategic analysis, planning, operational execution for a business unit | Strategy & Ops, BizOps Manager, Director Strategy |
| **Operations Manager / Director / VP** | Functional ops within a department (Sales Ops, Marketing Ops, Product Ops, Revenue Ops) | Director Sales Ops, Director Marketing Ops |
| **COO** | Org-wide operational leadership | Chief Operating Officer |

**Critical distinctions:**
- **TPM ≠ PM:** TPMs own cross-team delivery and technical project orchestration; PMs own product strategy and roadmap.
- **Program Manager ≠ Project Manager ≠ Product Manager:** All three exist. Program Manager owns ongoing cross-functional programs. Project Manager owns time-bound projects (more common at agencies/consulting). Product Manager owns a product.
- **Chief of Staff overlap with Strategy & Ops:** Both involve strategic initiatives. CoS is closer to the CEO/founder; Strategy & Ops is closer to a specific business unit.

| **Staff / Principal Designer** | Senior IC designer; sets design direction for a product area | Staff Designer, Principal Designer |

## How to tell which function a role actually is

**From the JD body, not the title:**

- Does it talk about roadmap, requirements, prioritization → Product Manager
- Does it talk about positioning, packaging, launches → Product Marketing
- Does it talk about brand voice, narrative, campaigns → Brand Marketing
- Does it talk about workflow, briefing, agency mgmt, studio operations → Creative Operations
- Does it talk about channel mix, CAC/LTV, paid acquisition → Growth Marketing
- Does it talk about email, CRM, push, retention loops → Lifecycle Marketing
- Does it talk about cross-team technical delivery, dependencies, milestones → Technical Program Manager
- Does it talk about quota, pipeline, closing deals → Sales
- Does it talk about strategic initiatives reporting to CEO → Chief of Staff
- Does it talk about org-wide operational planning → BizOps / COO

**From the team:**
- Does the role manage engineers? → Engineering Management
- Does the role manage PMs? → Product Management leadership
- Does the role manage creatives? → Creative function
- Does the role have no direct reports? → Likely IC
- Does the role manage cross-functional people from multiple departments? → Program Management

**From the reporting line:**
- Reports to CMO/VP Marketing → Marketing function head
- Reports to CPO/VP Product → Product Management
- Reports to CTO/VP Engineering → Engineering
- Reports to COO → Operations
- Reports directly to CEO → Senior exec OR Chief of Staff

**From the comp band:**
- Engineering tends to pay more than equivalent-level PM or marketing at FAANG
- Sales has heavier variable comp (commissions)
- Operations / Program Mgmt tends to be middle-band
- Creative leadership pays similar to marketing at the same level

## Common conflations to avoid

1. **PM ≠ PMM** — Product Management owns roadmap; Product Marketing owns GTM. Different jobs.
2. **TPM ≠ PM ≠ Program Manager** — Three different roles. TPM is technical+delivery, PM is product strategy, Program Manager is cross-functional coordination.
3. **EM ≠ IC SWE** at senior levels — Engineering Manager and Staff Engineer are parallel tracks, not "more senior" versions of each other.
4. **Brand Marketing ≠ Brand Design ≠ Creative Director (IC)** — All have "brand"/"creative" in titles but are different functions.
5. **Director Product Management ≠ Director Product Marketing** — Same word "Director" + similar word "Product" — different functions.
6. **"Senior" at FAANG vs startups:** Senior Engineer at Google = ~6-9 yrs experience. Senior at a startup might mean "the first non-junior person." Comp bands differ wildly.
7. **Chief of Staff** is its own function — overlaps with Strategy & Ops, BizOps, Program Mgmt, but distinct because of proximity to the CEO/founder.

## When in doubt

Ask the user: **"What's the most specific name for the function you do? Not 'I'm in tech' or 'I'm in marketing' — what specific role family. And what would the people who do the same job at other companies call themselves?"**

Their answer narrows the function quickly. If they hesitate, walk them through the function tables above and have them point to the closest match.
